# Ishmail Beene

## About Me

I'm a healthcare data analyst and Data Science student at Oregon State University. At work, I build and maintain production data pipelines in BigQuery and Dataform, and I built the insights data model my organization uses to make decisions. That work showed me how much depends on clean, reliable data, and it's what pulled me toward data science. I want to go beyond preparing data to modeling it, using predictive and inferential methods to answer real questions directly.

Before working in data, I served in the Peace Corps. My counterparts there didn't speak English, so I built programs in Thai. That taught me to communicate across differences and to lead without formal authority, and I still rely on both when I work across teams.

At OSU, I'm pursuing the Advanced Data Science option to build a stronger foundation in statistics, mathematics and machine learning. Outside of work and school, I'm a new dad, which keeps me focused on using my time well. I'm especially interested in healthcare, where good models can directly improve patient outcomes.

## Career Goals

My near-term goal is to move from data analysis into a data scientist role. I work with production data systems every day, but most of my work ends once the data is prepared for others. I want to be the one who builds the predictive and inferential models that answer the question directly, and who can explain and defend the results.

To get there, I'm working on three things. The first is my OSU coursework in statistics, mathematics and machine learning, which gives me the theory behind the tools. The second is projects I can show publicly, starting with a gradient boosting model in BigQuery ML, so my skills are visible beyond my job. The third is learning to validate models and test them for bias, which matters in healthcare because a model's errors affect real patients.

Longer term, I want to move from task-level work to system-level work, designing machine learning systems rather than individual models. I'm open to applying that in healthcare, pharma or finance. I plan to pursue a master's degree after I graduate to deepen my foundation, because I want my knowledge to genuinely match my credentials.
